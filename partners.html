SENTRO

Sentro ng mga Nagkakaisa at Progresibong Manggagawa (ITUC)

Major Philippine national labor center organizing transport and logistics couriers; co-leads tripartite DOLE negotiations and collective bargaining advocacy against unfair algorithmic deactivations.

ITF Asia-Pacific

International Transport Workers' Federation

Global democratic federation of 18M+ transport workers; coordinates international campaigns against multinational platform tech giants and supports cross-border labor rights.

🌏 Global network Accredited ally

Labor federation

KAGULONG

Kapatiran sa Dalawang Gulong (Riders Rights Network)

Pioneering Metro Manila two-wheel delivery couriers alliance; co-organizer of the May 1st Rider Unity Caravan, safety protocols, and mutual aid stations.

📢 May 1 co-convener Accredited ally

Legal defense

CTUHR

Center for Trade Union and Human Rights (Est. 1983)

Independent human rights NGO documenting platform labor abuses and providing free legal representation for riders unjustly deactivated or blacklisted by platform dispatch algorithms.

⚖️ Rapid-response legal Legal aid partner
📜
Legal defense

ILAW Network

International Lawyers Assisting Workers Network

Global network of trade union labor attorneys advising on statutory social protections, reclassification of gig riders, and algorithmic transparency challenges.

📑 Strategic litigation Legal aid partner
⚖️
Legal defense

AHRC Labor Desk

Ateneo Human Rights Center • Labor Justice Desk

University legal clinic providing pro-bono consultation, grievance mediation, and policy drafting support for the national Magna Carta for Delivery Workers.

🎓 Pro-bono clinic Legal aid partner

Academic research

Fairwork & UP SOLAIR

Fairwork Project & UP School of Labor and Industrial Relations

Academic initiative evaluating digital platform companies against international decent work standards, documenting net wages after fuel inflation and road hazards.

🔍 Decent work audit Research partner

Academic research

IBON Foundation

IBON Socio-Economic Research Institute

Independent research institute calculating the realistic family living wage (₱1,197/day in NCR) and documenting fuel cost inflation squeezing motorcycle courier take-home pay.

💰 Living wage data Research partner

Inter-faith and community alliance providing emergency monsoon sanctuaries, dry clothing, hot meals, and motorcycle staging rest stops along EDSA and Welcome Rotonda.
