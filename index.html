What We Are Demanding vs Platform Policy

Rainy Season Pay Cuts Hazard Pay
Current: Base delivery fee cut by 20% in rainy months.
Demand: +35% Monsoon Hazard Pay per wet-weather delivery.
Unrealistic Delivery Speeds Safety Timers
Current: Fixed 25-min timers regardless of flooded streets.
Demand: Dynamic timer adjustments during PAGASA rain alerts.
Arbitrary Account Suspension Due Process
Current: Algorithmic deactivation without human explanation.

Safety Gear & Medical Coverage Worker Health
Current: 100% equipment & injury expenses borne by rider.
Demand: Free company-issued rain gear & emergency clinic fund.

ACADEMIC & CIVIC FRAMEWORK

Battling Global Giants Locally (B.G.G.L.)

United Delivery Riders of Metro Manila (UDRMM) is a civic project developed by Taskforce 2 for MSM Assignment 2 under the campaign 'Battling Global Giants Locally'. Our goal is to illustrate how grassroots gig workers can organize against multinational platform exploitation.

By blending local cultural solidarity (bayanihan, barangay community networks) with modern digital campaigning and collective bargaining strategies, we prove that no corporate giant is too large to negotiate with organized labor.
